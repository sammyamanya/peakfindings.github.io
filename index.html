Peak Findings Ltd

Research & analytics to turn data into better decisions.

Peak Findings Ltd is a research and analytics firm based in Kigali, Rwanda. We support non-profits, development organizations, and institutions to design, analyze, and use evidence to improve decision making and service delivery.

What We Do

  • Impact evaluation and learning agendas
  • Applied data analytics and visualization
  • Research design and evidence synthesis
  • Decision support for programs and policy
